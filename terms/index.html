Service description

Rise & Capy is an alarm clock app for iPhone. It schedules alarms using Apple's AlarmKit, offers optional wake-up missions before an alarm can be dismissed, and tracks a simple local wake streak. The app runs entirely on your device — there is no account system and no backend service beyond what's needed to process your purchase.

Alarm reliability

Rise & Capy schedules alarms through Apple's AlarmKit framework, which is designed to sound through Silent mode and most Focus modes, the same way the built-in Clock app does. However, we cannot guarantee that an alarm will sound under every possible condition — for example, if your device is powered off, out of battery, in Airplane Mode without the app configured correctly, or affected by an iOS or hardware issue outside our control.

Please test your alarm setup on your own device before relying on it for anything important, and do not rely on Rise & Capy as your sole method of waking up for safety-critical situations.
