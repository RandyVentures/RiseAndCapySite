Questions people actually ask

The short version of a few common ones — the full list is on the FAQ page.

Is Rise & Capy a subscription?

No. It's a single $9.99 purchase for lifetime access — no trial that quietly converts, no recurring charge.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no server. Your alarms, preferences, and streak are stored locally on your iPhone.

Will it wake me up through Silent mode?

Yes. Alarms are scheduled through Apple's AlarmKit — the same system behind the built-in Clock app — so they ring through Silent and most Focus modes.
