FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

The short versions. The full list is on the FAQ page, or email us.

Is Rise & Capy a subscription?

No. It's free to start with one alarm. Capy Pro is a single $9.99 purchase that unlocks unlimited alarms and every mission for life. There's no trial that quietly converts and no recurring charge.

Will it wake me up through Silent mode?

Yes. Alarms are scheduled through Apple's AlarmKit, the same system behind the built-in Clock app, so they ring through Silent and most Focus modes.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no server. Your alarms, preferences, and streak are stored locally on your iPhone.

Can I turn wake-up missions off?

Yes. Each alarm's mission is optional. Set it to "None" if you'd rather just tap to dismiss.

Does Capy speak my language?

Probably! The app and Capy's spoken wake-up lines come in 16 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, and Portuguese.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later. There's no iPad layout, Apple Watch app, or Android version yet.
